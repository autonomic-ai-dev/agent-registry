# Playbook: Entrepreneur Journey (`entrepreneur`)

> **Trigger & Intent:** Discussing a business idea, startup concept, customer discovery, market validation, MVP scoping, manual processizing, pricing strategy, first 100 customers, sustainable growth, or company values.
> **Deliverable:** A lean, minimalist business advisory roadmap and concrete action deliverable grounded in Sahil Lavingia's *The Minimalist Entrepreneur* framework.

---

## 2. Core Invariants

1. **Sell Before You Build:** Validation requires a transaction, not compliments. Never recommend writing code before manual verification with real potential buyers.
2. **Processize Before Productizing:** Document manual workflows on a "magic piece of paper" (spreadsheets/emails) before automating.
3. **Weekend MVP Constraint:** If a prototype cannot be built in 2–3 days, strip features until it can.
4. **Charge from Day One:** Zero price gives zero signal. Mandate pricing from the first transaction.
5. **Skip the Launch:** Prohibit fake "viral launches"; mandate 1-on-1 direct sales to the first 100 customers.
6. **Profitability as Infinite Runway:** Always design business models to be profitable on day one without external capital.

---

## 3. Checklist Steps

1. **Stage Calibration:** Identify the founder's exact location in the 10-stage journey:
   - *Community -> Validate -> Processize -> MVP -> First Customers -> Pricing -> Marketing -> Grow Sustainably -> Culture -> Review*
2. **Deep-Dive the Relevant Stage:** Load the corresponding reference file from `skills/entrepreneur/references/<stage>.md`.
3. **Interrogate Root Pain:** Ask the 4 critical questions before building:
   - Can it ship in a weekend?
   - Does it make customer life a little better?
   - Will someone pay for it today?
   - Can feedback be gathered immediately?
4. **Produce Concrete Artifacts:** Generate tangible deliverables:
   - *Stage 1:* Community evaluation matrix and 10 member targets.
   - *Stage 2:* Problem statement, workaround analysis, and validation verdict.
   - *Stage 3:* The "magic piece of paper" manual workflow (Trigger, Steps, Tools, Time, Handoff).
   - *Stage 4:* Weekend MVP specification (1 core form, 1 list, 0 fluff).
   - *Stage 5:* Concentric circle outreach list and personalized cold templates.
   - *Stage 6:* Pricing model (cost-based vs value-based) and breakeven customer count.
   - *Stage 7:* Content marketing curriculum ("teach everything you know").
   - *Stage 8:* Profitable unit economics checklist and hiring discipline rules.
   - *Stage 9:* 3–5 non-obvious company values written as stories.
   - *Stage 10:* Minimalist review verdict across complexity, profitability, and maintenance.
